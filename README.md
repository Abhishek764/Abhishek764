## ✦ Know About Me

<img align="right" src="Flor ASCII Art.jpg" width="220" alt="Flor ASCII Art"/>

**Hey there! I'm Abhishek**

CS undergrad fueled by caffeine and a deep obsession with building things that matter. I navigate the worlds of **Web Dev**, **Cloud**, **DevOps**, **AI/ML**, and **Cybersecurity** — not because I have to, but because the intersection is where the interesting problems live. By day I'm designing systems; by night I'm questioning every decision I made designing them. I believe the best code is the kind that survives your future self reading it at 2am — and I'm still working on writing that code.

I find meaning in open-source: shipping real work into the world, not just side projects that die in private repos. Whether it's containerizing chaos with **Docker**, orchestrating it with **Kubernetes**, or making sense of data with **ML pipelines** — I chase the problems that matter. Still learning. Always building. Occasionally breaking things in production and pretending it was intentional.
